  correction/manual work, what the coding agent did to recover, and whether it
  could then proceed independently.
- Impact, final state and what verification established. Mock/unit-test success
  isn't live-service or deployment proof.

timings, opinions or evidence. Record what helped. No friction is valid;
don't force a problem or an NVIDIA angle to qualify for a reward.
